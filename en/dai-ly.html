Frequently asked questions

Can I mix tread patterns in one order?
Yes. One order can include several tread patterns and tyre sizes.

How are warranty claims handled?
3-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of purchase from Dương Gia. Dealers receive claims and forward them to Dương Gia; response within 7 working days.

Do you issue VAT invoices?
Yes. Prices include VAT, and Dương Gia issues VAT invoices for wholesale orders and business customers.
