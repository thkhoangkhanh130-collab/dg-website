About us

Dương Gia

Tyre importer and distributor — your partner on every road.

Who we are

Dương Gia imports truck tyres directly from the factory and distributes them to dealers, garages, fleets and transport companies.

Dương Gia is an authorised distributor of WYNSTAR tyres in Vietnam and runs a tyre service store in Đắk Lắk: tyres, wheels, batteries, lubricants and tyre services.

Our principles

Fair prices. Honest quality. Promises kept. There are no shortcuts to a long-term business.

How we work

Transparency

Published list prices, a clear dealer policy and full VAT invoices for wholesale orders and business customers.

Official channels

Direct import, full documentation, quality inspection under Vietnamese technical regulations.

Partnership

We support dealers from tyre selection and ordering to delivery and warranty.
