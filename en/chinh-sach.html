Prices & payment

  • List prices on this website are in Vietnamese dong (VND) per tyre, including value-added tax (VAT) and excluding any shipping fee. Prices may change; the applicable price is the one on the quotation or order confirmed by Dương Gia.
  • Prices and stock status on the website are correct at the time of viewing; quantities are subject to actual stock when Dương Gia confirms the order. Any promotion states its period, eligible customers and conditions.
  • Payment: bank transfer to the account in the name of CÔNG TY TNHH THƯƠNG MẠI DỊCH VỤ XNK DƯƠNG GIA (account details on the order), or cash at our store.
  • Dương Gia issues VAT invoices for wholesale orders and business customers.
  • Dealers: payment and credit terms follow each dealer tier’s policy, as shown on the order.
  • Dương Gia never asks for payment into a personal account. If you are told our bank account has changed, please call +84 973 929 321 to verify before paying.

Delivery

  • Nationwide delivery within Vietnam to the customer’s address; the delivery fee depends on the address and is quoted before the order is confirmed.
  • Free delivery within Ho Chi Minh City (former city boundary).
  • Delivery method: Dương Gia’s own trucks or partner carriers; the carrier and estimated delivery time are stated on the order.
  • On delivery, please check quantity, size and tread pattern; notify Dương Gia of any discrepancy within 5 working days of receipt.

Warranty & exchanges

  • 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of purchase — details on the Warranty & technical.
  • Dương Gia replaces goods delivered in the wrong size, wrong tread pattern or wrong quantity against the confirmed order, if reported within 5 working days of receipt; Dương Gia bears any extra shipping cost when the error is ours.
  • Refunds (when both parties agree not to exchange): transferred to the paying account within 9 working days of the agreement.
  • Tyres that have been fitted or used cannot be returned or exchanged, except under warranty.

Complaint handling
