Warranty & technical

Warranty policy for WYNSTAR tyres distributed by Dương Gia, how to read tyre markings and safe-use guidance.

Warranty policyWarranty processHow to read tyre markingsUse & maintenance

Warranty policy

  • Period: 3 years from the date of purchase from Dương Gia.
  • Scope: damage caused by manufacturing defects (materials or workmanship).
  • Applies to: WYNSTAR tyres distributed by Dương Gia, with proof of purchase (invoice or order).
  • Not covered: damage from under- or over-inflation, overloading, wrong wheel position or rim, impacts, cuts from sharp objects, normal wear, or improper repairs.
  • Outcome: based on Dương Gia’s assessment with the manufacturer.

Warranty process

  1. Report the defect via the dealer you bought from, or hotline / Zalo 0973 929 321.
  2. Send tyre photos (tread, sidewall, damaged area), the DOT code on the sidewall and proof of purchase.
  3. Dương Gia assesses the claim with the manufacturer.
  4. Dương Gia gives its decision within 7 working days of receiving the complete claim.

How to read tyre markings

Example size 12R22.5 18PR 152/149 L:

12Nominal section width of the tyre, in inches.
RRadial construction.
22.5Rim diameter, in inches.
18PRPly Rating — a load-capacity index equivalent to a number of plies; not the actual number of plies.
152/149Load index: the first number is for single fitment, the second for each tyre in dual fitment (152 = 3,550 kg; 149 = 3,250 kg).
LMaximum speed symbol (L = 120 km/h).

For a metric size such as 315/80R22.5: 315 is the section width (mm) and 80 is the sidewall height as a percentage of the width. A size ending in LT (e.g. 7.50R16LT) indicates a light-truck tyre.

Use & maintenance

  • Check pressure when tyres are cold and inflate according to the manufacturer’s load–pressure table.
  • Never exceed the load that corresponds to the tyre’s load index.
  • Dual-fitted tyres should be the same size and tread pattern, with similar wear.
  • Regularly check tread depth, cuts and bulges; rotate tyres according to the vehicle’s service schedule.
