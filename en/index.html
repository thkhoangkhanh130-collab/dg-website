Ordered directly from the factory, with certificates of origin (C/O), customs declarations and VAT invoices for every shipment.

Import capability

From factory to warehouse, a fully documented route

Dương Gia runs its own import chain: ordering, shipping, customs clearance and quality inspection. No middlemen, so we control both quality and price.

1

Factory orders

Direct contracts with the manufacturer, selecting patterns, sizes and ply ratings for the Vietnamese market.

2

Document checks

Goods are checked against the contract, C/O, invoice and packing list before container loading.

3

Shipping

Sea containers to Ho Chi Minh City port, with vessel schedules and each shipment tracked.

4

Customs clearance & inspection

Customs declaration, testing registration and quality inspection under current Vietnamese regulations.

5

Warehousing & delivery

Goods are warehoused, stock status is updated in the app, and orders are delivered to dealers and customers.
