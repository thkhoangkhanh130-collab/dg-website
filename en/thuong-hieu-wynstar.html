Brand we distribute

All-steel radial truck tyres from Double Coin Group (Chongqing) Tyre Co., Ltd. Dương Gia imports directly and distributes WYNSTAR in Vietnam.

Factory

Double Coin Group (Chongqing)

WYNSTAR is a brand of Double Coin Group (Chongqing) Tyre Co., Ltd. — a key project of Shanghai Huayi Group and Double Coin Group, located in the Shuangqiao Economic and Technological Development Zone, Chongqing, China.

The factory supplies vehicle makers such as Yutong and JAC and exports to more than 80 countries, including Vietnam. Total investment exceeds RMB 1.8 billion on a site of almost 1,000 mu (about 67 hectares).
