Operating conditions & warranty

Right tyre, right place, clear warranty

Wheel position & road type

Wheel position: semi-trailer axle. The manufacturer states it is not for steer or drive axles.

Road type: paved roads, mixed roads.

Pressure & maintenance

Inflate to the pressure in the manufacturer’s load–pressure table (Single load and Dual load columns above). Check pressure when tyres are cold, rotate tyres regularly and never exceed the design load.
